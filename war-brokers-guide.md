# War Brokers - Complete Guide (2026)

War Brokers is a free, browser-based first-person shooter featuring vehicles, weapons, and large-scale combat.

## Quick Info

- **Type:** Browser-based 3D FPS
- **Cost:** Completely Free
- **Platforms:** Browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) + Steam
- **Players:** Thousands online daily
- **Game Mode:** Battle Royale, Team Deathmatch, Missions
- **Official Site:** warbrokers.io

## How to Play

### Option 1: Browser (No Download)
1. Go to **warbrokers.io**
2. Click "Play Now"
3. Create account or login
4. Select game mode
5. Start playing instantly

### Option 2: Steam
1. Search "War Brokers" on Steam
2. Click "Play Game" (free)
3. Download via Steam client
4. Launch and play

## Game Modes

### Battle Royale (BR)
- **Players:** Up to 100 per match
- **Goal:** Last player standing wins
- **Duration:** 15-20 minutes
- **Features:** 
  - Loot scattered across map
  - Shrinking play area
  - Vehicle combat (helicopters, tanks, APCs)
  - Air, land, and sea warfare
- **Best For:** Players who like survival gameplay

### Team Deathmatch (TDM)
- **Format:** 8v8 or 16v16 teams
- **Goal:** Eliminate enemy team members
- **Duration:** 10-15 minutes
- **Features:**
  - Respawning players
  - Multiple maps
  - Vehicle availability
- **Best For:** Fast, action-packed matches

### Missions (PvE)
- **Format:** Squad-based cooperative
- **Goal:** Complete objectives vs AI
- **Duration:** 10-20 minutes
- **Features:**
  - Team coordination required
  - Various difficulty levels
  - Rewards and progression
- **Best For:** Players who want PvE content

### Special Events
- **Easter Zombie Battle Royale** - Fight zombies while battling players
- **Alien Invasions** - Survive alien attacks
- **Seasonal Events** - Holiday-themed modes with special rewards
- **Limited-Time Modes** - Rotating events with unique mechanics

## Tips & Strategies

### Beginner Tips
✓ Start in Training Mode to learn controls
✓ Master one weapon before switching
✓ Use headphones to hear enemy footsteps
✓ Play Team Deathmatch to practice without BR pressure
✓ Learn map layouts before competitive play

### Battle Royale Strategy
✓ Land away from other players initially
✓ Loot efficiently (grab weapons and armor first)
✓ Stay in safe zone as it shrinks
✓ Use vehicles strategically (positioning > holding)
✓ Listen for helicopters and gunfire
✓ Take high ground for advantage
✓ Don't waste ammo on distant targets

### Combat Tips
✓ Headshots are critical hits (more damage)
✓ Peek corners before running into open
✓ Use vehicle cover to block incoming fire
✓ Coordinate with teammates in missions
✓ Learn weapon spray patterns
✓ Switch weapons when ammo runs low
✓ Communicate enemy positions to team

### Advanced Techniques
- **Headglitch:** Position so only your head is visible
- **Vehicle Bouncing:** Jump from moving vehicles for positioning
- **Spray Control:** Practice weapon recoil management
- **Timing Rotations:** Move with the safe zone before panic
- **Baiting:** Use teammates to reveal enemy positions

## Known Issues & Solutions

**Game won't load?**
- Clear cache: Ctrl+Shift+Delete
- Try incognito mode
- Disable extensions temporarily
- Use different browser

**Lag/Stuttering?**
- Lower graphics quality in settings
- Close other browser tabs
- Check internet connection
- Move closer to router

**Controls not responding?**
- Click game window to focus
- Rebind keys in settings
- Try gamepad instead of keyboard
- Update browser

**Can't connect to server?**
- Check internet connection
- Verify firewall settings
- Try VPN (if allowed)
- Wait a few minutes and retry
